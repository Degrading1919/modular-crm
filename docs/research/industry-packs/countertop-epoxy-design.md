## Executive summary

This profile treats the candidate as decorative epoxy application/resurfacing on existing kitchen or bathroom counters, including faux stone/veining and clear protective topcoat. It is distinct from epoxy flooring, custom solid-surface fabrication and the separate Trades/construction combined candidate. **Observed evidence:** a Calgary contractor explicitly defines epoxy resurfacing as coating the existing counter; manufacturer directions identify prep/priming compatibility for several substrates, mixing/application steps and care limitations. Work is sensitive to substrate condition, edges/sink cutouts, masking, design approval, cure and ventilation.

**Inference:** The CRM opportunity is a project estimate and multi-step job record with counter dimensions/photos, existing surface, design sample approval, product batch, application and cure/return notes. **Uncertain:** standalone trade maturity, common software and operator WTP remain thinly evidenced. Sources disagree on whether suppliers' DIY countertop kits translate into a reliable contractor service or maintenance expectation. Marked partial because no strong, independent operator panel or incumbent business software usage/WTP evidence was found.

## Business model and customer segments

Residential homeowners, rental-property owners and remodelers may buy a lower-disruption decorative refresh over a serviceable existing counter. Contractor may also offer cabinet painting. Jobs include kitchen/bath vanity tops, island surfaces, backsplash or separate repair/recoat, with an on-site decorative finish application. A service may be contractor-branded around hand-designed marble/metallic finishes, not factory-fabricated stone. Exclude structural repair, sink/plumbing replacement, fabricated stone and flooring unless separately contracted. Sales may come from search/social portfolio, referrals, remodelers and quote requests; demand and longevity are not quantified.

## Terminology and durable records

Record customer, address, room and counter segments; countertop substrate (laminate, wood, solid surface, stone, other), current coating/condition, dimensions/area, edges/backsplash, sink/cooktop/cutout and plumbing handoff, design/colors/veining, sample approval, primer/base/color/topcoat products and batch, ambient conditions per product instructions, masking, cure/re-entry instructions, before/after photos, warranty and care. Countertop is a persistent property fixture with product and finish history; damage, cleanliness, exact area and access are job-specific. Capture food-contact/heat/scratch use expectations and exclusions accurately; manufacturer cautions customers about hot pans/trivets, and “epoxy” does not mean heat-proof or scratch-proof.

## Services, intake, quoting, and booking

Potential services: consultation/sample, surface repair and sanding, bonding primer/sealer, base coats, decorative color/veining, flood/clear coat, topcoat, backsplash, hardware/sink masking and limited recoat. Intake should request photos, counter area/measurements, substrate, current finish, chips/cracks/delamination, sink/cooktop/backsplash, desired appearance, occupancy/pets, access, water shutoff/plumbing responsibilities and target dates. A site assessment is prudent for adhesion/condition; sample panel/color signoff before full application reduces expectation mismatch. Quote can use minimum plus surface area/linear edge, substrate/prep/repair, complex design, vertical/backsplash, masking, material, sink hardware removal/reinstall coordination and return visits. Booking is after condition/design review, not unconditional instant purchase.

## Pricing and commercial model

**Observed service-price evidence:** EpoxyCountertop.ca in Calgary says quote depends on kitchen size, condition and finish; JW Epoxy Counters publishes an overview including $200–$600 for countertop sanding/refinishing, but does not establish an all-in national epoxy countertop rate. A Reddit founder/operator discussion about entering existing-counter epoxy work says costs include materials, overhead and margin and cites roughly 35 sq ft and $300–$400 material in one region; it is an old, single self-reported planning discussion rather than verified market pricing. Quote variables should remain area, substrate/repair, design complexity, masking, product and return/cure visits. Do not treat product-kit prices as installed service prices.

**Software pricing/WTP:** No countertop-epoxy software product/plan or operator subscription purchase statement found. WTP is unconfirmed. The contractor's service quote and material cost do not establish software budget.

## Recurrence, scheduling, dispatch, and routing

Typically a one-off home improvement project, possibly requiring prep/application and a later inspection or return visit. Work schedule depends on client clearance, product-specific cure time, ambient conditions, ventilation and plumbing/sink coordination. Furniture/room access and temporary kitchen/bath availability affect start. Route planning is generally unnecessary for one crew/project; a larger finish contractor may schedule multiple sites. Recoat or repair recurrence is condition-driven, not a subscription default; do not promise cure/access timing beyond the selected product label.

## Field workflow, safety, completion, and rework

Confirm scope and existing damage; protect floors/cabinets/walls; remove/cover fixtures as agreed; verify substrate and product compatibility; prep/sand/degrease and remove dust; prime/seal as specified; record sample approval; accurately ratio/mix resin and hardener; apply base/design/flood coat and remove bubbles per product method; protect against dust/contamination; observe cure; inspect color/edges/bubbles/fisheyes/pinholes/level and adhesion; document photos/product and care/cure; obtain acceptance and schedule rework if needed. Manufacturer TDS specifies exact mixing ratio, mixing, coat depth and handling steps; the chosen product's SDS controls PPE. OSHA documents skin contact/sensitization risk for uncured epoxy; ventilation/PPE are task and product dependent. Use gloves/eye protection, suitable work clothing and respiratory/ventilation controls as indicated in SDS/risk assessment; open flame/heat gun use in a household requires hazard control and manufacturer adherence. Confirm food-contact suitability and local disposal rules for selected system; do not claim all epoxies are safe for direct food contact. Rework includes bubbles, dust, uneven color, poor adhesion, sticky/soft cure from mix error, yellowing, scratches, edge failure or heat damage.

## Customer communication and self-service

Request photos and dimensions, explain prep/access and occupancy expectations, show approved sample and finish limitations, confirm sink/plumbing responsibilities, communicate cure/re-entry/ventilation instructions, send arrival/delay, change approvals, completion images, care instructions, warranty and invoice. Automated marketing claims should not state universal scratch/heat resistance or countertop suitability beyond the exact manufacturer's documentation. Online flow is a quote request, with design/condition review before confirmed scope.

## Equipment, inventory, suppliers, and workforce

Sanders/abrasives, dust extraction, cleaners/degreasers, compatible primer/sealer, epoxy resin/hardener, pigments, measuring/mixing containers, spreaders/squeegees, brushes/rollers, controlled heat source for bubbles if product calls for it, masking/containment, PPE, ventilation and drop protection. Materials are system- and substrate-specific; track lot/ratio and expiry. A lead finisher may use a helper for masking/application; some operators combine cabinet painting. Plumbing/electric disconnection should be clearly assigned to qualified subcontractor/customer, not silently included. Do not assume bulk inventory or any particular payroll pattern.

## Reporting and operating measures

Track leads with requested finish, quote acceptance, measured surface, prep labor, material batches/cost, cure/return timing, sample approval, on-time finish, callbacks by defect/condition, rework hours, warranty and gross margin. These are inferred useful measures. No trade-wide published benchmarks were found.
