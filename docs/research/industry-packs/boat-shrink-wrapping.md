# Boat shrink wrapping

## Profile metadata

- **Industry key:** `boat-shrink-wrapping`
- **Source name:** Boat shrink wrapping
- **Source category:** Home services
- **Candidate source:** [Businesses I Love](https://www.sweatystartup.com/blog/businesses-i-love), Home services, position 72 (after “Boat cleaning”; before “Boat repair/maintenance”).
- **Other exact/near duplicate labels:** Boat cleaning (position 70), boat repair/maintenance (72), and RV / boat winterizing (73) are separate profiles. This is seasonal installation/removal and disposal of a protective shrink-film enclosure; it can be sold alongside winterization/storage but does not mean the vessel’s systems were winterized.

- **Geographic scope:** US marina/yard pricing and US Northeast clean-marina/environment examples; local fire, waste, yard and storage policies vary.

## Executive summary

**Observed evidence:** Operators sell shrink wrap mainly by measured length or area, with price adjustments for boat shape/height, supports, vents, doors, vessel location (in-water vs yard), and material. The work is seasonal and often paired with haul-out, storage, winterization, or spring removal, but these are separately priced services. BoatUS warns the torch process can start a fire, and that a sealed cover traps moisture; ventilation, support-frame design and safe tie-downs are critical. Maryland and Connecticut clean-marina guidance promotes recycling/controlled collection of used film.

**Inference:** A high-value workflow is a measured vessel job with site/yard access, materials, frame and ventilation inspection, photo proof, customer approval for extras, and spring removal/recycling follow-up. **Uncertain:** observed pricing is regional and operators vary in whether they include frame, vents, door and removal; the candidate label alone does not establish how many businesses specialize in wrapping only.

## Business model and customer segments

Boat owners, marinas, boatyards, dealers and storage operators buy a fall installation before outdoor layup. The crew may install at a marina slip, lift, yard, trailer or customer property; winter removal may be a separate spring job. A marina can sell this as a seasonal service menu, while specialist crews may cover multiple yards. Project timing is seasonal and weather sensitive; storm damage or tear repair is an exception, not the core offering.

## Terminology and durable records

Persistent record: vessel/owner, HIN, make/model/year, measured overall length/beam/height, hull/deck geometry, cockpit/cabin/rails, installed canvas/antenna/accessories, trailer/cradle/stands, storage site/slip/yard, access/permission, prior wrap and known contact-sensitive finishes. Job inputs: material gauge/color, frame layout, vents, access door, tape/cord, date and weather. Track location and support system because a cover must not pull on jack stands/support blocks. Keep boat systems service history/winterization separate.

## Services, intake, quoting, and booking

Core: survey and measure, prepare boat (owner/crew responsibility defined), build frame/supports, apply/tension/secure film, add ventilation and optional access door, inspect edges/clearances, capture completion photos, and later remove/collect film. Quote inputs include actual wrap length/area, vessel geometry and height, existing rails/arch, in-water/lift/yard access, frame material and labor, vents/door, haul-out, travel, removal and recycling. Intake asks for photos, dimensions if known, site contact, yard permissions, date/storage arrangement and whether winterization is already contracted. Site survey should override customer-supplied dimensions when risk/material estimate warrants. Do not advertise a winterized boat unless that separate service was actually completed.

## Pricing and commercial model

**Observed US service price evidence:** Highland Lake Marina’s 2026 rate card lists $20/ft up to 25 feet and $22/ft for 26–30 feet (jet ski $10/ft) and separate haul-out/winterization; its page also describes storage as separately priced ([rate card](https://www.marinahighlandlake.com/winter-service), accessed 2026-09-24). Barker’s Island Marina’s 2025–26 form says wrap price is based on measured overall shrink-wrap length ([rate sheet](https://barkers-island-marina.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rates-BIM-2025-2026-Shrink-Wrap-Rates-R0-082725.pdf), accessed 2026-09-24). An Argentina Marine 2025/26 menu states included brace/caps/tape/cordage/vents/mildew bag and separately priced in-water boats/access doors ([menu](https://www.argentinamarine.com/fckimages/pages/service/2025%20_2026%20Winterizing%20Shrink%20Wrap%20updated%202_23_25.docx.pdf), accessed 2026-09-24). Prices/geography and exact inclusions differ.

Model per measured length/area or tier, plus shape/height, frame/support, vents/door, in-water/haul-out, labor/travel, removal/recycling, tax and unusual site access. Deposit and cancellation/weather policies are operator-specific. Software WTP: no operator statement or observed subscription purchase for shrink-wrap companies was located. Generic field-service apps or marina systems have published/demo pricing, but this is not direct WTP; the small seasonal specialist may tolerate phone/calendar/invoice tools or pay for work-order scheduling only if crew/yard coordination justifies it.

## Recurrence, scheduling, dispatch, and routing

Fall peak begins as boats leave service and storage slots fill; sequence depends on haul-out/yard schedule and suitable weather. Book by promised layup date, site readiness and crew/material capacity. Cluster boats at the same marina/yard to reduce travel and crew setup; track yard access and the order of haul-out, wrap and storage. Schedule removal/recycling in spring or on customer request. Wind/rain/temperature can affect install quality; reschedule and notify rather than hold a fixed arrival guarantee that crew cannot meet.

## Field workflow, safety, completion, and rework

Confirm permission, worksite, measurements, boat condition and wrap scope; photograph existing finish/support arrangement; install stable frame independent of stands/blocks; protect contact-sensitive surfaces; use torch/film with trained crew and fire precautions; install adequate vents/door, secure cover and check water shedding/clearances; record materials, measurements, photos, crew and exceptions; get customer/yard acceptance. BoatUS warns inexperienced shrink wrapping has caused fire and ventilation prevents trapped moisture/mildew; frames prevent pooling and the cover must not be tied to jack stands/support blocks. Establish fire extinguisher and hot-work site rules with marina/yard; local fire codes/permits not researched universally. Document tears, frame movement, loose edges, trapped moisture complaints and callback response. Film removal must protect vessel and be staged for local collection/recycling; Connecticut and Maryland programs are examples, not nationwide mandates.

## Customer communication and self-service

Send quote, scope/inclusions, preparation list, site access/yard approval, arrival/weather change, finished photos, inspection notes, winter access door location and spring removal instructions. Offer winterization/storage as visibly separate add-ons, with approval. Portal stores vessel measurements and repeat-job photos, but remeasure after modifications or different storage/site conditions. A generic “winter ready” claim can mislead; state only what was contracted.

## Equipment, inventory, suppliers, and workforce

Film rolls, tape/cord, vents, zipper doors, caps, frame stock, straps, shrink-wrap torch/heat equipment, PPE, fire extinguishers, ladders/platforms, knives, work vehicle, and disposal/collection bags. Inventory demand rises before fall and is size/gauge/color dependent. Crew skills: measure vessel and frame, safe torch use, ventilation, weather judgment and yard safety/access. Crew count and subcontractors vary with boat size. Material lot, roll usage, reusable frame inventory, and returns/waste are useful for costs; proprietary frame methods remain operator specific.

## Reporting and operating measures

Track quote-to-book by marina, measured feet/area, material/labor per job, setup/travel, on-time completion before storage date, rework/tear/weather callbacks, vent/door/frame add-ons, removal/recycling completion and seasonal gross margin. These are inferred operating measures; no sources establish an industry-wide KPI standard.

## Existing software and operator evidence

Marina operators publish PDF forms/rate sheets and use service calendars, work orders, vessel/customer records, yard/storage schedules, phone/email, payment/accounting and spreadsheets. DockMaster markets marina service work orders and vessel records, though no public list price was found. Specialist wrap crews may use a general field-service calendar plus invoicing; this stack remains uncertain. BoatUS guidance supplies safety/quality criteria; rate cards demonstrate measured-length pricing/inclusions. No reliable direct operator software complaint or software WTP evidence found. Seasonal specialist software need should not be inferred from customer service ticket value.

## Evidence, disagreements, and uncertainty

**Observed evidence:** Public marina rate sheets use per-foot or measured-length pricing and vary in what is included. BoatUS highlights fire, ventilation, mildew, frame and support risks. Connecticut/Maryland examples support film recycling programs. **Inference:** measurement, frame/vent checks and reuse of vessel records reduce repeat quoting and rework. **Uncertain:** installation can be a specialist service or marina add-on; disposal rules and pricing differ by region; software use/WTP are not documented. Confidence high for workflow/safety distinction, medium for product fit, low for software adoption.

## Handoff to a future pack implementer

Ask for boat size/classes, measurement method, yards served, install/removal scope, frame/material/vent/door options, disposal plan and any marina fire/permit rules. Prototype a measured quote, yard access, crew checklist/photo signoff, weather reschedule and spring removal reminder. Keep pricing, frame methods, recycling arrangements and venue policies configurable. Test whether current forms/attachments can retain dimensions, support layout and evidence before claiming a new platform gap.
