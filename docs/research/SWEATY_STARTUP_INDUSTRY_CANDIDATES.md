Other near-identical labels include **Custom closet build-outs / Closet build out**, **Mobile car mechanic / Mobile mechanic**, and **Porta Potty rental / Porta potty rentals**; verify whether their operations materially differ before any later consolidation. Differently worded entries for **Residential Painting / Painting**, **Moving/Hauling / Moving services**, and **Thermal imaging - utility saving / Home inspection / thermal imaging** link to the same respective article on the source page. Their labels remain in the inventory and may share a profile only if the profile calls out both entries and their distinctions. Related service labels such as interior/exterior painting, deep kitchen/restaurant cleaning, junk removal, and party/tent rentals are deliberately not deduplicated here.
